About Circuitry

Circuitry is a place for inventors to share ideas, get honest feedback and find the people who can help build them. Whether you have a sketch on paper or a working prototype, you can post it here.

Intellectual Property (IP) & Patent Guide

  • Your ideas belong to you. Posting on Circuitry does not transfer any rights.
  • A public post can count as public disclosure, which may affect a later patent application. Talk to an IP adviser first if you plan to patent.
  • Share the idea, but keep sensitive technical details private until you are protected.
  • Always credit people whose work you build on.

Guidelines & Submission Rules

  • Post your own work, or say clearly whose work it is.
  • Pick the correct category and stage.
  • Give feedback that is kind, specific and useful.
  • No spam, adverts or unsafe instructions.
